## What Growth Circles are

Starting is one thing; keeping going is another. Growth Circles bring founders
together in small, consistent groups so they can solve problems out loud, hold
each other to commitments, and learn from people a few steps ahead.

## Who it is for

Founders with a business that has been trading for at least a year, who want to
grow without losing what already works.

## How it works

- Small groups of eight to ten founders
- A monthly facilitated session, plus a lighter check-in in between
- Guest mentors on specific challenges: hiring, pricing, record-keeping, export
- Optional one-to-one mentoring for founders who want it
