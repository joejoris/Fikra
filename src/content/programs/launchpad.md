## What Launchpad is

Launchpad takes founders who have proven there is demand and helps them build a
business around it. Each founder is matched with a mentor, given a working space,
and supported to reach their first consistent revenue.

## Who it is for

Founders who have completed **Idea Lab** or can demonstrate an equivalent tested
idea, and who are ready to commit to building full-time or near full-time.

## What you get

- A dedicated mentor who meets you every week
- Desk space and reliable internet at the FIKRA hub
- A small startup grant to cover early costs
- Monthly clinics on finance, marketing and operations
- Introductions to partners and potential customers

## What is expected of you

Launchpad is not passive. You are expected to work on your business every week,
report honestly on progress, and support the founders around you.

## How to apply

Applications open cohort by cohort. [Get in touch](/contact) to register your
interest and be notified first.
