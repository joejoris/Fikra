## What Idea Lab is

Idea Lab is where FIKRA starts. Over six part-time weeks, participants take a raw
idea and pressure-test it against the people it is meant to serve. There are no
lectures for the sake of lectures — every session ends with something to go and
try in the real world.

## Who it is for

Young people who have an idea and the drive to test it, but who have not yet
started a business. No prior experience or qualification is required.

## What you will do

- Identify a real problem and the customer who feels it most
- Talk to potential customers and learn from what they say
- Sketch a simple business model and price it
- Build a first version of your offer and get feedback
- Present your idea to mentors, peers and partners

## What you leave with

A validated idea, a one-page business model, a first customer conversation, and
a clear next step — either into **Launchpad** or back to the drawing board with
better questions.
