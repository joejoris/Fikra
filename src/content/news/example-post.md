This is an example news post. It is marked as a **draft**, so it will not appear
on the live website — it only exists to show how publishing works.

## How to write a post

1. Open the admin panel (see the admin guide).
2. Choose **News**, then **New post**.
3. Fill in the title, a one-line description, the date and your name.
4. Write the body using the editor.
5. Switch the **Draft** toggle off when you are ready to publish.

## Writing tips

- Lead with the most important sentence.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Link to the relevant programme or page where it helps.

Once you save, the site rebuilds automatically and your post goes live within a
minute or two.
